Privacy Policy

SP Avatr does not collect, transmit, or store any personal information.

Last updated 28 August 2026

What the app stores on your device

The app saves three things, all on your iPhone only:

  • The last local network address your vehicle's head unit answered on, so it can be found again quickly.
  • Your light/dark theme preference.
  • A cryptographic key pair, generated on your device at first launch and held in the iOS Keychain, used to authenticate the connection to your own vehicle.

None of this is sent anywhere. Deleting the app deletes all of it.

Network activity

The app communicates with your vehicle's head unit over the local Wi-Fi network your phone and your vehicle are both connected to, and with this site's public release folder to check whether a newer version of the car's service exists.

That check sends nothing about you or your vehicle — it is an ordinary request for a public file. No server operated by the developer receives your data, because none is ever sent.

What we do not do

No analytics. No crash reporting. No advertising. No tracking of any kind. No third-party SDKs. No accounts and no sign-in. We never receive your data because none is ever sent.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects nothing from anyone.
