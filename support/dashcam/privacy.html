Privacy Policy — SP Dashcam

SP Dashcam collects nothing. There is no account, no analytics, and no server operated by the developer. Your phone talks to your vehicle and to nothing else.

Applies to the iOS and Android app SP Dashcam. Last updated 4 September 2026.

Data we collect

None. We operate no server, run no analytics, and have no way to receive information about you or your vehicle. Nothing you do in this app is reported to us or to anyone else.

What the app stores on your device

Three things, all held only on your phone:

  • The web address your vehicle publishes its recordings on.
  • The access token from the code you scanned, which is what lets your phone read your vehicle's recordings. It is a credential — treat the pairing link the way you would a password.
  • Your light/dark appearance preference.

Deleting the app deletes all three.

Where the app connects

To one address: the one your vehicle published when you paired, and which you approved by scanning its code. Requests carry the access token so your vehicle knows the phone is yours.

That connection travels over the internet through the tunnel your vehicle opens, so it works away from the car. It is encrypted in transit. The developer operates no part of it and can see none of it.

The app contacts no other server. There are no third-party SDKs of any kind.

Your video

Recordings stay on the memory card in your vehicle. The app streams them for viewing and does not copy them to your phone unless you choose to export a clip — and an exported clip goes into your device's photo library and nowhere else. It is never uploaded.

Permissions

Camera
Only to scan the pairing code your vehicle displays. Nothing is recorded, kept or transmitted; the camera preview is discarded as it is read. The app works without this permission — you can paste the pairing link instead.
Photos (add only)
Only to save a clip you have chosen to export. The app requests add-only access, so it cannot read, browse or see the rest of your library.

The app requests no location permission, and has no location code of any kind.

What we do not do

No analytics. No crash reporting. No advertising or ad identifiers. No tracking, and no sharing or selling of data — we hold none to share. No accounts and no sign-in. No third-party SDKs.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects nothing from anyone.

Changes

Any change to this policy will be published on this page with a new date.
