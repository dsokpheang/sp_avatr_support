Privacy Policy — SP Remote

SP Remote collects nothing. There is no account, no analytics, and no server operated by the developer. Your phone talks to your vehicle and to nothing else.

Applies to the iOS and Android app SP Remote. Last updated 8 November 2026.

Data we collect

None. We operate no server, run no analytics, and have no way to receive information about you or your vehicle. Nothing you do in this app is reported to us or to anyone else.

What the app stores on your device

  • The web address your vehicle answers on, and the access token from the code you scanned. It is a credential — treat the pairing link the way you would a password.
  • Your vehicle's addresses on Wi-Fi, so the app can find it again directly.
  • A random identifier made by the app when installed, so your vehicle can remember that you allowed this phone. It is not linked to you, your device or any other app.
  • Your favourite commands, the vehicle's command list and its app icons.

Deleting the app deletes all of it.

Where the app connects

Only to your vehicle: over the internet through the tunnel your vehicle opens, or directly when your phone is on the same Wi-Fi as the vehicle. Requests carry the access token so your vehicle knows the phone is yours. The developer operates no part of it and can see none of it.

A place, song or video you search for in the app is sent to your vehicle, which runs the search in its own apps. The app contacts no other server. There are no third-party SDKs of any kind.

Siri

If you use Siri with SP Remote, Apple handles your voice under Apple's own privacy policy. The app receives only the resulting request and sends the matching command to your vehicle.

Permissions

Camera
Only to scan the pairing code your vehicle displays. Nothing is recorded, kept or transmitted. The app works without this permission — you can paste the pairing link instead.
Local Network
Only to reach your vehicle directly when your phone shares a Wi-Fi network with it. The app looks for your vehicle and nothing else on that network.

The app requests no location permission and reads no photos, contacts or files.

What we do not do

No analytics. No crash reporting. No advertising or ad identifiers. No tracking, and no sharing or selling of data — we hold none to share. No accounts and no sign-in. No third-party SDKs.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects nothing from anyone.

Changes

Any change to this policy will be published on this page with a new date.
