SP Avatr

Tools for Avatr & Deepal cockpit head units

Support Privacy License

SP Avatr keeps the companion service on your car's head unit running, and keeps the on-screen tools up to date — from your phone or a laptop, in the car, without a cable.

The apps

SP Avatr — iPhone

Finds the head unit on Wi-Fi, checks the service, restarts it, and pushes the newest build. No laptop, no cable.

SP Service Starter — Mac & Windows

Two buttons. Start service brings the service back after a reboot; Unlock does the same on a car that needs the diagnostic cable first.

SP Avatr Kit — on the car

The controller for everything on screen: keyboard switching, battery level, voice commands, karaoke, and updates for the whole suite.

SP Store — on the car

Search for an app, check it, install it — on a head unit that refuses ordinary installs.

Releases

Every app updates itself from the release folder. You do not need to download anything from here by hand — open the app and it fetches what your car is behind on. Each file is checked against a published SHA‑256 before it is installed, so a partial or substituted download is refused rather than deployed.

Something not working? The support page covers what actually goes wrong: the car not being found, the service stopping after a power cycle, and the head unit refusing a phone's key.
