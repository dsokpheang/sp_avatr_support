Tools for the cockpit.

Apps for Avatr and Deepal head units — watch the car's recordings from your phone, follow it on a map, and keep the on-screen tools current.

Software

SP Dashcam — iPhone & Android

The footage your car is already recording, on your phone. Scan the code the centre screen shows, then browse, play and save loop and sentry clips. Support · Privacy

SP GPS Tracker — iPhone & Android

Your vehicle on a map, its drives replayed, and zones that tell you when it arrives or leaves — reading from the Traccar server you run. Support · Privacy

SP Service Starter — Mac & Windows

Two buttons. Start service brings it back after a reboot; Unlock does the same on a car that needs the diagnostic cable first.

SP Avatr Kit — on the car

The controller for everything on screen: keyboard switching, battery level, voice commands, and updates for the whole suite.

SP Store — on the car

Search for an app, verify it, install it — on a head unit that refuses ordinary installs.

Releases

Every app updates itself from the release folder. Nothing here needs downloading by hand — open the app and it fetches what your car is behind on.

Each file is checked against a published SHA‑256 before it is installed, so a partial or substituted download is refused rather than deployed.

Help

The support page covers what actually goes wrong: the car not being found, the service stopping after a power cycle, and the head unit refusing a phone's key.
